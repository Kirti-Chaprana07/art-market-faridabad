Our Heritage & Vision

About Art Market Faridabad

Preserving centuries of Indian brass & wood handicraft traditions while bringing modern zero-maintenance greenery to contemporary homes.

Rooted in Faridabad, Cherished Across India

Founded in Faridabad, Haryana, Art Market Faridabad was born out of a profound admiration for traditional Indian artisanal metalwork and timeless decor aesthetics.

In a world dominated by mass-produced plastic knick-knacks, we curate genuine solid brass statues, temple lamps, antique railway station clocks, and hand-carved jharokhas that carry soul, weight, and history.

Why Artificial Botanicals?

We believe greenery brings life to every living room and workspace. However, busy urban lifestyles, lack of sunlight in high-rises, and maintenance challenges often make real plants difficult to sustain.

Our curated Real-Touch Artificial Plants & Bonsais provide the exact optical texture, leaf veining, and lush aesthetic of living plants without requiring watering, soil changes, or pest control.
