Frequently Asked Questions

Where is your Faridabad store located and what are the timings?

Our showroom is located at Sector 15 / Main Mathura Road, Faridabad, Haryana. We are open all 7 days from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. You can visit anytime or schedule a VIP visit with free parking.

Are your brass and antique items made of solid metal?

Yes! All our brass idols, urulis, lamps, and clock frames are cast in heavyweight solid brass and seasoned metals with natural antique patina finishes, not hollow lightweight plastic.

How do I order directly through WhatsApp?

Simply click the "WhatsApp" button on any product card or in your cart. It opens WhatsApp with your selected item name, price, and SKU pre-filled so you can confirm instant delivery with our shop owner!

Do you offer corporate gifting and bulk decor packages?

Yes! We specialize in customized corporate Diwali gifting, housewarming hampers, and full interior decor packages for cafes, clinics, and offices across Faridabad and Delhi NCR.
